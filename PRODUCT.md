# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian creators, founders, products, brands, people, and legitimate organisations use GoneViral to understand and purchase public sponsored placement. Visitors need to scan exact ranks, totals, and current takeover prices quickly. Sponsors need financial language that is precise enough to trust before any checkout exists.

## Product Purpose

GoneViral is a public sponsored leaderboard where confirmed cumulative sponsorship, net of applied reversals, determines position. The board is the product. Success means a visitor understands the paid ordering within five seconds, can see real database-backed positions and amounts, and never mistakes an estimate, browser return, or synthetic fixture for confirmed activity.

## Brand Personality

Premium, provocative, trustworthy. The voice is culturally current without slang overload, recognisably Indian through currency and language rhythm, and calm enough to handle exact money.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a casino, betting product, trading terminal, crypto product, purple or blue gradient SaaS dashboard, neon dark-mode interface, glassmorphic component kit, fake live auction, tricolour brand exercise, or a copied Outbid or Million Dollar Homepage layout. Avoid giant hero sections, fake counters, fake urgency, excessive crowns, flames, confetti, and generic component-library styling.

## Design Principles

1. Put the truthful board and exact amounts before decorative marketing.
2. Disclose paid ordering wherever a ranking appears.
3. Treat estimates, stale data, empty states, and pending legal work honestly.
4. Use dense editorial hierarchy and familiar controls so the interface disappears into the task.
5. Let real confirmed data create energy. Never manufacture activity or urgency.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support semantic board table and list structures, visible focus of at least 2px, complete keyboard navigation, 44px touch targets, 200% text zoom, 320px layouts without horizontal page scroll, reduced motion, colour-independent meaning, and accessible INR labels.
